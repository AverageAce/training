Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Training is a personal workout log made for one person. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what it does with data.

Google account data

With your permission, Training reads and writes one Google Sheet that you own: your training log. It uses the Google Sheets scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets) only for that Sheet. It never reads other files, your email or your contacts.

The iPhone app keeps its Google sign-in token in the iPhone Keychain. It is never sent anywhere except Google.

Apple Health data

With your permission, the iPhone app reads sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, workouts and walking or running distance from Apple Health. It copies a daily summary of those values into your own Google Sheet. It never writes to Apple Health.

AI analysis

A script that runs in your own Google account can send a day's log and health summary from the Sheet to Anthropic's Claude API to write a short training analysis back into the Sheet. Nothing is sent unless you turn this on and add your own API key.

What Training does not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone else.
  • No storage on servers of its own. Your data stays in your Google account, your iPhone and, when you turn on AI analysis, Anthropic's API.

Training's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Remove Training's access at any time in your Google Account under Security › Third-party apps, and in the Health app under Sharing › Apps.
